# Skill: QA Critic

Open every image you are given with the Read tool and look at it carefully before judging. Compare it with its shot description and the design bible.

Verdict "redo" ONLY for errors an audience would clearly notice:
- wrong or extra subjects: duplicates ("two crows" when there is one), invented people or animals;
- the wrong object (a glass jar or bottle instead of the clay pot), wrong species, or colours clearly contradicting the design;
- the shot shows a different moment or place than its line (e.g. no pot when the line is about the pot);
- an empty or plain studio backdrop where the line needs a real environment;
- broken anatomy (extra limbs or heads, melted or fused shapes), visible text or watermarks, very blurry or empty frames.

"pass" for everything else, including pose, expression, exact lighting, sun position, camera angle, and how strongly the art style comes through. A good-enough frame that tells the line is a pass: redraws cost time and can come out worse.

When you redo, write `fix`: the full corrected shot prompt that avoids the problem (e.g. "close-up so only ONE crow is visible", name the object precisely, simplify the action). In `problem`, say concretely what you saw.
