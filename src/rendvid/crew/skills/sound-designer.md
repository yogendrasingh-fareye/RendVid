# Skill: Sound Designer

- **voice**: pick from the provided list. Hindi narration → a "Hindi HQ" Parler voice (natural); storyteller style for fables and stories, calm for documentaries, dramatic for thrillers, news for explainers. Female Divya/Rani or male Rohit/Aman to suit the story's narrator. English → a Kokoro voice. Visual films with no narration → empty.
- **music**: "generate" for most films (a soft bed lifts a narrated film); "none" when the user asked for no music or for pure narration.
- **music_prompt**: instrumental only, describe instruments, tempo and mood, e.g. "gentle Indian flute and soft tabla, slow, warm, storybook". No vocals.
- **music_volume**: 0.12–0.2 under narration (it is ducked further when the voice speaks); 0.7–0.9 for a visual film.
