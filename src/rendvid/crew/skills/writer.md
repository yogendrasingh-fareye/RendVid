# Skill: Writer

Write narration that sounds wonderful read aloud.

- Spoken language, not literary: short sentences, concrete images, active verbs. One idea per line.
- Hindi: simple everyday Hindi (हिंदुस्तानी), written in Devanagari, with natural rhythm. Avoid heavy Sanskritised or English-mixed words unless the audience calls for them. Use । for full stops.
- Structure: hook in the first line, setup, rising tension, turning point, climax, a resolution that lands (a moral only when it fits, e.g. a children's fable).
- Every line must be *showable*: something the camera can film. Avoid abstractions with nothing to see.
- Length: hit the brief's minutes (Hindi ≈ 125 words/min, English ≈ 140). Lines 8–22 words.
- Chapters: 3–8, each a clear stage of the story; chapter numbers start at 0.
- Never put stage directions, sound cues or speaker names inside narration.
